Mortgage Processing FAQ

How quickly do you start processing a file?

Most files are picked up the same business day they're submitted. Once we have a complete file, our processors begin document review right away so your pipeline doesn't lose momentum.

How quickly are loan estimates generated?

Loan estimates are typically generated within three hours of receiving a completed file.

What areas do you serve?

We work with mortgage brokers throughout California.

Which loan origination systems do you work with?

We work inside the loan origination systems brokers already use, including Encompass, Calyx Point, LendingPad, and other industry-standard platforms.

Do you work directly with borrowers?

No. We work exclusively with brokers and loan officers, not directly with borrowers, so you remain the point of contact with your clients throughout the process.

Can you handle multiple loan files at the same time?

Yes. Our pricing is structured around monthly file volume, from 1–25 files a month up to 50+, so handling multiple files at once is built into how we work.
