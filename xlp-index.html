Mortgage Processing FAQ

How quickly do you start processing a file?

Files are reviewed immediately after submission by a lead processor and built or uploaded within 1-2 hours of receipt.

How quickly are loan estimates generated?

Loan estimates are typically generated within three hours.

What areas do you serve?

We provide mortgage processing services for brokers and loan officers across California.

Which loan origination systems do you work with?

We work with several industry-standard LOS platforms including Encompass, Calyx Point, LendingPad, and Blue Sage, and can adapt to the workflow used by your brokerage or lender.

Do you work directly with borrowers?

We offer flexible communication tailored to your workflow. While we prefer engaging directly with borrowers to ensure a seamless and efficient experience from start to finish, we are more than happy to step back and support Loan Officers with borrower communications upon request.

Can you handle multiple loan files at the same time?

Absolutely. Xpress Loan Processing is unique in that we have developed a cutting edge, proprietary AI-powered system that allows our team to process high volumes of loan files with ease. XLP supports brokers and loan officers with both small pipelines and high-volume loan processing. Whether you submit a few files per month or dozens of loans, our team can scale to meet your processing needs.
